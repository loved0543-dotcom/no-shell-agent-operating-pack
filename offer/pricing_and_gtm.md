# Future Pricing And GTM Hypothesis

Current mode: free public beta. Do not activate payments, storefronts, checkout links, or paid upsells until there is clear public usage signal.

## Product ladder
1. Free public beta: live MCP/API, diagnostic card, and starter workflow pack.
2. Future Starter Pack hypothesis: $29 for one polished domain workflow pack.
3. Future Operator Bundle hypothesis: $99 for five workflow packs + scorecard + recovery playbook.
4. Future Custom Workflow Audit hypothesis: $300 for one customer workflow turned into a concrete AI operating brief.

## First workflow niche
Start with "email and document automation for solo operators." It is broad enough to understand, but narrow enough to demonstrate.

## First experiment
- Audience: 20 people who already use ChatGPT/Claude but failed to automate work.
- Ask: show the free public beta, then ask them to try one real workflow and leave a GitHub issue or direct feedback.
- Pass condition: 5 real workflow attempts, or repeated stars/issues from relevant users.
- Kill condition: people say it is just prompts, or cannot use the first 10-minute card without help.

## Sales route
- Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy/Ko-fi style digital file sale only after public beta signal is strong.
- Reddit/Discord/LinkedIn posts with before/after screenshots.
- YouTube/short blog demo: "AI automation failed because the prompt missed input-output-recovery."

## What not to sell yet
- Anything during the current public beta.
- Monthly consulting.
- Done-for-you unlimited automation.
- Live account actions.
- Tool installation guarantee across every customer device.

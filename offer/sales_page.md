# Sales Page Draft

## Headline
Stop asking AI to "automate this" and getting empty shells.

## Subheadline
A plain-language operating pack for non-coders who use ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, Gemini, or Hermes and want real workflows: goal, tool choice, commands, validation, recovery, and handoff.

## Korean headline
AI한테 자동화 맡겼는데 껍데기만 나온 적 있으면, 이 팩은 그걸 막는 운영표입니다.

## What You Get
- 10-minute automation intake.
- Tool and plugin router in plain language.
- Copy-paste command cards.
- No-shell scorecard.
- Failure recovery playbook.
- Delivery checklist for your first real workflow.

## Who This Is For
- You already use AI tools but do not know which plugin, skill, or workflow to choose.
- You are not trying to learn programming first.
- You want one repeatable way to turn a task into a working AI-assisted process.

## Who This Is Not For
- People who want a full custom agency build.
- People who want guaranteed account integrations without logging in.
- People who want AI to send money, publish, or change accounts without review.

## CTA
Start with one workflow. If it cannot pass the no-shell scorecard, do not automate it yet.
